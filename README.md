## 👋 Hi, I'm Caio

I'm a **data scientist** who loves **machine learning and AI in general**, from building models to AI engineering. I also work with **ocean science data**, where ML meets real-world problems.

## 🎓 Education

- **MSc, Earth and Atmospheric Sciences**: University of Alberta (2026 to now)
- **BSc, Information Systems**: University of São Paulo (2021 to 2025)

## 🌊 What I'm working on

- **Thesis:** identifying dominant flow patterns in the **Hudson and James Bay** region by applying **unsupervised machine learning** (K-means++, K-medoids, DBSCAN, hierarchical clustering) to Lagrangian trajectory data.
- **Lagrangian particle tracking:** 3D simulations with `Parcels` forced by high-resolution (1/60°) **NEMO-ARC60** ocean and sea-ice fields.
